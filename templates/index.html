Edge = Profit %

(resale × (1 − seller fee) − (face + primary fees)) ÷ (face + primary fees), per ticket. It ranks by upside only and doesn't account for the odds of actually getting tickets. The events with the highest edge are usually the hardest to buy.

Live vs Estimated

Live is based on asking prices: the median SeatGeek listing once 10+ listings exist, less 15% because resale usually sells below the ask. Estimated predicts a resale multiple of face from a demand score (Last.fm listeners and plays per listener, listeners per venue seat, tour size, market) and shows a range. Confidence is Med when Last.fm knows the artist and Low when it doesn't; without Last.fm data an event can't be rated High demand. A High demand · est. label means the rating relies on an estimated venue capacity, used only when the show would still rate High in a 3,000-seat room. Estimated events sort by the midpoint of their range.

Estimates are uncalibrated

Tier weights and multiple ranges are placeholder guesses until enough predictions have been scored against real resale prices. Hover or tap a Live or Estimated label to see the inputs behind each number.
