Edge = Profit %

(resale × (1 − seller fee) − (face + primary fees)) ÷ (face + primary fees), per ticket. It ranks by upside only and doesn't account for the odds of actually getting tickets. The events with the highest edge are usually the hardest to buy.

Live vs Estimated

Live uses the median SeatGeek listing once 10+ listings exist. Estimated predicts a resale multiple of face from a demand score (Spotify popularity, followers per venue seat, tour size, market) and shows a range. Estimated rows sort by the midpoint of their range.

Estimates are uncalibrated

Tier weights and multiple ranges are placeholder guesses until enough predictions have been scored against real resale prices. Hover or tap a confidence label to see the inputs behind each number.

Presales and face values from the Ticketmaster Discovery API, live resale from SeatGeek, artist popularity from Spotify. Refreshed every 6 hours. Always confirm times and prices before a sale.
